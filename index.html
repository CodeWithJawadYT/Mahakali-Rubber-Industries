Manufacturing Process

From drawing to dispatch — five controlled stages.

Every part follows the same traceable flow, with sign-off gates at tooling and first article.

01

Drawing Review & DFM

Engineering reviews your PDF/STEP, flags mould-ability risks and recommends the elastomer grade.

02

Compounding

In-house mixing to target hardness, chemical and temperature resistance, with batch rheometer checks.

03

Tooling

CNC-machined compression, transfer or injection moulds — 10–15 days typical.

04

Moulding & Finishing

Vulcanising, deflashing, cryogenic trimming and rubber-to-metal bonding as required.

05

QC & Dispatch

100% visual and dimensional inspection, material certificates and door-delivery logistics.

Elastomers & Materials Guide

The right polymer for the right media.

Hardness 30–90 Shore A. FDA and food-grade compounds available on request.

EPDM

Weather, ozone and steam resistant. -45°C to +150°C. Ideal for water, HVAC and outdoor seals.

NBR / Nitrile

Excellent oil and fuel resistance. -30°C to +120°C. Pumps, hydraulics, automotive seals.

Viton (FKM)

Aggressive chemicals and high heat up to +200°C. Oil & gas, chemical dosing, aerospace.

Silicone (VMQ)

-60°C to +200°C, FDA-compliant grades. Food, pharma and medical sealing applications.

Natural Rubber (NR)

Best abrasion and tear resistance. Plucking fingers, bushings, anti-vibration mounts.

Neoprene (CR)

Balanced weather, oil and flame resistance. Bellows, boots and general industrial seals.

FAQ

Answers before you ask.

What rubber grades do you manufacture in?
EPDM, NBR, Nitrile, Viton (FKM), Silicone, Natural Rubber, Neoprene and SBR — hardness 30–90 Shore A per drawing. Food-grade and FDA-compliant compounds available on request.
Do you produce custom rubber-to-metal bonded parts?
Yes. We produce bonded bushings, mounts and diaphragms with steel, brass and aluminium inserts using compression, transfer and injection moulding with chemlok bonding systems.
What is your minimum order quantity?
MOQs are flexible — prototypes from 25 pcs, production runs from 500 pcs. Tooling is quoted per drawing and amortised over the first run.
How fast can you deliver samples?
Typical prototype lead time is 10–15 working days once the drawing and material grade are confirmed. Rush tooling available on request.
Which industries do you serve?
Automotive, agriculture & poultry processing (defeathering fingers), oil & gas, HVAC, water treatment, food processing and general industrial OEMs.
